Price drops: questions people ask

What is a price drop?

A price drop is when a product's price falls below what it usually sells for. This page lists the drops our tracker has just found on Amazon and Flipkart, newest first.

What does "New low" mean?

The price just fell to the lowest we have ever recorded for that product, from a higher price.

What does "Below usual price" mean?

The price is well under the product's long-run average price, even if it has been lower before.

How often do you check prices?

Products are checked through the day, and new drops appear here as soon as they are found. The time at the top shows the newest one.

Can I get an alert for one product?

Yes. Tap the bell on any deal, or open the product and set the price you want. The Price History app and extension can also send the alert to your phone.
