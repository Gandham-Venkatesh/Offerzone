# Pages read straight from the stores:   URL | price | label     (price and label are optional)
#
# PRODUCT PAGE   The price is your TARGET: you are alerted when the product reaches it. It also alerts
#                when the product falls 50% or more below the price this monitor has been seeing for it.
#
# CATEGORY, BRAND or SEARCH PAGE   The price is a FLOOR: "nothing on this page should cost this little".
#                Use a page sorted cheapest-first. A Rs 60,000 laptop mispriced at Rs 600 becomes the
#                cheapest laptop on the store, so it shows up at the top of that one page. This is how a
#                price error is caught at the store itself, with no deal channel involved.
#                  - The first read of a page only records what is normal on it. After that, anything NEW
#                    at or under the floor alerts, and so does anything that FALLS to it.
#                  - A NEW item under the floor counts only if its list price (MRP) is at least 3x the
#                    floor (FLOOR_MRP_RATIO): toy laptops and phone cases stay quiet.
#                  - Accessories, spares and refurbished units are skipped.
#                  - Every product on the page is also checked against traps.txt and its own price history:
#                    a fall of 50% or more from the price it was seen at, saving Rs 1,000 or more, alerts
#                    quietly, loudly from 65%, at max priority from 80% (WATCH_DROP_PCT, URGENT_DROP_PCT).
#                  - A product the monitor has never seen, NEW on the page above the floor, alerts when its
#                    own card shows 85% or more off a list price of at least 3x the floor
#                    (NEW_LISTING_DISCOUNT): high priority, max priority from 95%. This is what catches a
#                    dropped digit, a Rs 74,990 laptop at Rs 7,499.
#                A page without a price is followed for its products' own price history only.
#
# HOW OFTEN   A store is asked for a page only every few seconds (Flipkart: every 3-5), so about a dozen
#             of its pages fit into one scan, and a longer list takes turns, longest wait first.
#               !! at the start of a line: the page comes round 12 times as often as an unmarked one
#               !  at the start of a line: 4 times as often
#               "| every 15m" at the end:  never more often than that (for whole catalogues)
#             With this file: the "!!" pages come round about every 2-3 minutes, the "!" pages about
#             every 7, the unmarked Flipkart pages about every half hour.
#
# Everything below was read correctly from GitHub's servers on 12 Nov 2026; each floor sits at roughly
# 40% of the cheapest real product the page showed that day. --doctor shows the cheapest item on each
# page and warns if ordinary products already sit under a floor.
#
# Not here because they refuse plain requests or build their pages only inside a browser: Amazon,
# Myntra, Snapdeal, Tata CLiQ, JioMart, Nykaa Fashion, Meesho, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart.
