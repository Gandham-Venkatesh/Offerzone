# Keyword traps:   keywords | max price
#
# If a deal post (or a product on a watched search page) names one of the keywords at or
# under the max price, you get an URGENT alert - even when the post gives no MRP and never
# says "price error". This is what catches "Nothing Phone @ 400".
#
#   - Separate alternative keywords with commas.
#   - Set the max price well below anything the product could normally cost.
#   - Accessories ("iPhone 16 case", "cover for Nothing Phone"), refurbished units and EMI
#     amounts are ignored automatically.
#   - Keep keywords specific. "oneplus nord" would also match the ₹1,599 "OnePlus Nord Buds";
#     "ps5" would match every ₹1,999 PS5 game.
#
# These are examples. Edit them to the things you would actually buy.

nothing phone, cmf phone | 6000
iphone | 12000
macbook | 20000
ipad | 6000
samsung galaxy s24, samsung galaxy s25, galaxy z fold, galaxy z flip | 10000
oneplus 13, oneplus 15 | 5000
gulab jamun, rasgulla | 20
